# 🛒 Amazon E-Commerce Sales & Order Fulfillment Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end data analysis of Amazon retail and order transactions. The objective is to evaluate overall revenue performance, identify top-selling product categories, analyze order fulfillment channels (FBA vs. Merchant/FBM), and uncover customer purchasing trends to help optimize inventory and reduce order cancellations.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered
- **Revenue Drivers:** Which product categories, sub-categories, and sizes contribute the highest share of gross revenue and order volume?
- **Fulfillment Performance:** How do Amazon-fulfilled orders (FBA) compare against seller-fulfilled orders (Merchant) in terms of order completion and return rates?
- **Geographic Demand:** Which states and metropolitan cities account for the highest concentration of buyers and gross merchandise value (GMV)?
- **Cancellations & Returns:** What percentage of total orders result in cancellations or returns, and how much potential revenue is lost?
- **Sales Trends:** What are the peak ordering times (day-of-week, hour-of-day, and month-over-month trends)?
